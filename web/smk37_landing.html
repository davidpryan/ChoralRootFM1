ChoralRoot for the SMK-37

Quixotic7's chord instrument on the M-Vave SMK-37, with the keyboard's 3.5 mm jack as a MIDI output: it plays an FM-1 running LOOM-8, or any synth, with no computer.

Version {{VERSION}} ({{DATE}}): a fork of ChoralRoot {{BASE}}. GPL-3.0. A community test build, not tested by ChoralRoot's maintainer.

With an FM-1 running LOOM-8

A 3.5 mm TRS cable from the SMK-37's jack into the FM-1's MIDI IN jack (or a type A adapter to a MIDI DIN cable for another synth). LOOM-8's tracks listen on MIDI channels 1 to 8: with the defaults the chord plays track 1 (MIDI Perform, channel 1) and the bass plays track 2 (MIDI Bass, channel 2). The wheels, the pedal and velocity go with the notes.

Tempo: set Options > MIDI Clock to Out on the keyboard and LOOM-8's CLOCK to receive, and LOOM-8's sequencer follows ChoralRoot's tempo and the loop's start and stop. Or leave the clock off and let LOOM-8 keep its own time. Options > TRS MIDI Out turns the jack off and on; USB MIDI keeps working beside it.

Before you install

Have M-VAVE's stock package for your model and their updater, M-UPGRADE, at hand: that is the way back. Keep the USB cable connected until the keyboard has restarted. The jack's setup follows the stock firmware as the porting guide read it and has not been confirmed on a unit by this project: if the keyboard's Options > TRS MIDI Out line reads UART not answering, please report it. Installing other firmware is at your own risk.
